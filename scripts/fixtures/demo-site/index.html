Trailhead Coffee Roasters

Trailhead Coffee Roasters is a small-batch coffee roaster based in Portland, Oregon. We source green beans directly from farms in three countries, roast in batches under twenty pounds, and ship every order within forty-eight hours so the coffee arrives at peak freshness.
Where do you ship?

Anywhere in the continental United States.

How fresh is the coffee?

We roast to order and ship within 48 hours.

Do you offer subscriptions?

Yes — weekly, biweekly, and monthly plans.
