About Us

Trailhead Coffee Roasters started in 2019 when two home roasters outgrew their kitchen setup and moved into a converted garage in southeast Portland. Today the same two people still run every roast by hand, tasting each batch before it ships to a customer.
Who roasts the coffee?

The same two founders roast every batch by hand.

Where are you located?

Southeast Portland, Oregon.

Do you roast to order?

Yes, every batch ships within 48 hours of roasting.
