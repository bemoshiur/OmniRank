Pricing

Trailhead Coffee Roasters sells three simple plans: a one-time bag starting at eighteen dollars, a biweekly subscription with free shipping included, and a wholesale tier for local cafes ordering more than twenty pounds of beans a month at a discounted per-pound rate.
Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, subscriptions cancel anytime from your account page.

Do you ship internationally?

Not yet — US only for now.

Is wholesale available?

Yes, for orders over twenty pounds a month.
